Supplementary Figure S3. Estimated Full gap and Raw-minus-method paired distributions.

(a) Estimated Full − Full translation error gap (mm) per trajectory, shown as median with descriptive IQR. Estimated Full (G2 est_warmstart arm) differs from Full by both view query and warm-start change in a single first pass; this is not a globally acquired initialization. Positive values indicate Estimated Full has higher error than Full. (b) Raw-minus-method paired translation error distributions, grouped by trajectory. Each trajectory group contains two boxplots: Full (orange) and Est. Full (gold). Boxes show IQR; central line is median; whiskers extend to 1.5×IQR. Dashed line at zero marks no benefit. Est. Full and Full are nearly coincident, as expected for the warm-start arm. All statistics are frame-paired and descriptive (IQR, not bootstrap CI).
