Fraction of frames meeting error thresholds (e_t ≤ 50 mm and e_R ≤ 2°) across seven discrete initialization levels (reference, 10/0.5, 30/1, 50/2, 100/5, 200/10, 300/15) by trajectory. VI and IV remain at zero capture across all levels (preserved, not threshold-swapped). Right column shows continuous translation error (median ± IQR) for the same levels. Primary comparison is Raw vs Patch per the E1 pre-registration; Huber/PatchHuber show the same pattern and are noted but not overlaid. All levels use fixed frame sets, direction, and run denominators.
