# Typography and export

Arial preferred, Liberation Sans/Helvetica fallback. Axis labels 9 pt; ticks and legends 8 pt; panel headings 9 pt bold; panel letters 10 pt bold. Curves 1.5-1.8 pt; uncertainty marks 1-1.2 pt; axes 0.85 pt. White background. PDF embeds TrueType; SVG outlines glyphs for portable appearance. PNG 600 dpi is a preview; PDF is the vector submission master. Mathematical subscripts, superscripts and log-axis exponents are naturally rendered smaller than their 8-11 pt parent text.
